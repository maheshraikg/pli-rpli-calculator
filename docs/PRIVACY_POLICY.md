# PLI & RPLI Calculator — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

PLI & RPLI Calculator is an independent calculator for India Post's Postal Life Insurance
(PLI) and Rural Postal Life Insurance (RPLI). It is **not** an official India
Post app.

## What we collect
Nothing. PLI & RPLI Calculator has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking. The app does not request the internet permission.

## What stays on your phone
- Policies you save under **My Policies** (holder name, policy number, plan,
  sum assured, dates, premium) are stored only on your device.
- Your language choice and whether you have seen the first-launch notice.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. It is never sent anywhere.

## Notifications
If you turn on premium reminders, the app schedules local notifications on
your phone. No server is involved.

## Sharing
When you share a quotation (PDF or image), the file is created on your phone
and handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). What happens after
that is governed by that app's policy.

## Optional rate updates
Builds configured with a rate-update address can download updated rate tables
over HTTPS when you tap **Check for rate updates**. Only the rate files are
downloaded; nothing about you is sent. The standard build has this turned off.
