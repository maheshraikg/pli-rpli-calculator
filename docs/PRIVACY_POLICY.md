# Premium & Maturity Calculator — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

Premium & Maturity Calculator ("the app") is an independent calculator for India
Post's Postal Life Insurance (PLI) and Rural Postal Life Insurance (RPLI).
It is **not** an official India Post or Department of Posts app and is not
affiliated with the Government of India.

## Summary
The app collects **no personal data**. It has no account, no analytics, no
advertising and no tracking, and it does not request the internet
permission. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

## What is stored on your phone
- **My Policies:** policy holder name, policy number, plan, sum assured,
  dates, premium, the customer's mobile number (optional), reminder setting,
  and which dues you marked as collected.
- **Agent profile (optional):** your name, phone, agent ID / office and a
  photo or logo you choose.
- **Settings:** your language and whether you have seen the first-launch
  notice.

This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is
never sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Backup files
If you tap **Back up policies**, the app creates a backup file on your phone
and opens the Android share menu. You choose where it goes (for example your
own WhatsApp chat or Google Drive). The file contains the My Policies data
listed above. Restoring reads a backup file you pick.

## Sharing and reminders
- **Quotations, letters and forms** you share are created on your phone and
  handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). What happens after
  that is governed by that app's privacy policy.
- **WhatsApp reminders** open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message to the
  customer's number; nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.
- **Premium reminders** are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No
  server is involved.

## Links to official websites
The **Official links** screen opens Government of India websites (such as
pli.indiapost.gov.in, indiapost.gov.in, digilocker.gov.in and
pgportal.gov.in) in your browser, and can dial the India Post toll-free
number. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Permissions
- **Notifications**, **vibrate** and **run at startup**: only to show
  premium reminders you turned on, including after the phone restarts.
- The app does **not** use the internet, location, contacts, camera,
  microphone or phone-call permissions. Choosing an agent photo or a
  backup file uses Android's system file picker, which gives the app only
  the file you select.

## Children
The app is intended for adults (insurance agents and policy holders) and is
not directed at children.
